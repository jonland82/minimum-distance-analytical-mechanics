Geometry

How large must a triangle be to survive?

Under the two sampling rules studied here, right triangles lose a larger fraction of shapes at every size above the cutoff. Very thin right triangles occur often enough to make their average required size infinite.

Algorithms

One-stop routing with a changing distance cutoff

Sorting possible stops once makes it faster to find the shortest route as the minimum-distance requirement changes. On the tested 100,000-stop workload, 1,000 queries ran 2.63 times faster including the index build.

Dynamics

How closely can a discrete space imitate continuum waves?

Waves on a discrete lattice can closely match waves in continuous space. Suitable stable interactions cancel progressively more of the slowdown as their range grows.
